About

I’m an Engineering Manager with a hands-on technical background and a strong bias toward execution. I specialize in translating ambiguous business goals into clear technical direction, managing cross-functional teams, implementing agile methodologies, building teams that scale sustainably, and delivering high-quality products.

I have led engineers across organizations such as Disney, Amazon, and NBCUniversal, partnering closely with product, data, and business stakeholders to deliver platforms used by millions. I have left my mark on products in web applications, streaming services, and mobile applications. My mission is to empower my teams to craft innovative solutions that cater to the diverse and global audiences we serve.

Colleagues know me as a highly creative professional who delivers innovative, simple solutions to complex business problems. I constantly learn about new technology and continue studying to better myself and my teams.

Scaling a Media Data Platform

Led a team responsible for a high-throughput platform supporting large-scale media distribution workflows. Focused on reliability, observability, and clear ownership boundaries.

  • Improved stability via on-call hygiene, alert quality, and operational playbooks
  • Reduced deployment risk by standardizing CI/CD and release patterns
  • Accelerated partner teams by clarifying APIs and service ownership

Growing and Structuring Engineering Teams

Built and scaled teams while improving clarity around expectations, performance, and technical decision-making.

  • Introduced structured feedback, goals, and growth frameworks
  • Improved onboarding and knowledge sharing to reduce single points of failure
  • Increased predictability through planning rituals and clearer priorities

Aligning Execution with Business Outcomes

Partnered with product and business leaders to prioritize initiatives that delivered measurable impact while protecting platform health.

  • Translated ambiguous goals into sequenced roadmaps with clear milestones
  • Balanced delivery speed with sustainable architectural decisions
  • Improved stakeholder confidence through transparent execution and tradeoffs
